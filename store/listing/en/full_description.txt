Angadi is a billing app for kirana, general and medical shops, made for Telugu-speaking shop owners. Say the items in Telugu, scan a barcode or tap a quick-sale tile, and the bill is ready. Your data stays on your phone or tablet: no account, no ads.

TELUGU FIRST
• The whole app in Telugu or English. Switch with one tap at the top.
• Items have a Telugu name, an English name and other names your customers use, and search finds any of them.

FAST BILLING
• Voice billing: tap Speak and say the items and quantities in Telugu. Uses your phone's speech recognition.
• Barcode scanner with the phone camera. No camera? Type the number. An unknown barcode can be linked to an item once and is found directly after that.
• Quick-sale tiles for the items you sell most.
• Sell by piece, pack, strip, kg, gram, litre or ml. Change quantities with + and −.
• Bill discount, and a void with a reason when a bill was wrong.

THREE WAYS TO GET PAID
• Cash: shows the change to give back.
• UPI: shows your shop's own UPI QR with the bill amount filled in. The money goes straight to your bank account. Angadi never touches it.
• Udhaar: the bill goes to the customer's account as dues.

GST RECEIPTS
• Prices include GST. Slabs 0%, 5%, 18% and 40%. Totals are rounded to the rupee.
• With a GSTIN, bills are tax invoices with the GST split. Without one, they are bills of supply.
• Receipt as a PDF, sent on WhatsApp or any app you choose. Bills of registered customers can go straight to their WhatsApp number.

CUSTOMERS AND DUES (UDHAAR)
• Each customer's dues, payments, promised pay date, previous bills and what they usually buy.
• Dues reminders on WhatsApp or SMS: Angadi writes the message, you tap send.

STOCK
• Stock goes down with every bill. Record stock in (purchase), damage and count corrections.
• Low-stock alerts at the level you set for each item.
• Stock value at selling price, and a check that the stock matches every recorded change.

REPORTS
• Day end: sales, bills, cash, UPI, udhaar, discount, GST and voids.
• GST summary for the month, top items and dues outstanding.
• Shop insights worked out on your phone from your own sales: items that will run out soon, and items not sold in 30, 60 or 90 days.

ALSO
• Notes for the shop (for example "order 2 bags of rice").
• Backup and restore: save a backup file to WhatsApp, Drive or anywhere you choose.
• Works on phones and tablets. Bigger screens show the items and the bill side by side.

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
Bills, customers and stock are stored only on your device. There is no Angadi server and no login. Data leaves the phone only when you share a receipt, send a reminder, or save a backup. If Android backup is on, Android also keeps a copy in your own Google account.

PRICE
50 bills free every month. Angadi Pro gives unlimited bills for ₹200 a month or ₹1,999 a year, paid through Google Play. Cancel any time in Google Play.
